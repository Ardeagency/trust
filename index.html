(04)(AI Governance)© 2026

Standing commitments

ARDE operates a human-directed production model: generative AI is a production tool operated under the direction, curation and final approval of human creative professionals.

  • Human review of every deliverableNo AI output reaches a client without direction, curation, editing and approval by ARDE's human team, followed by the client's own approval workflow.
  • No training with client dataClient materials, data and deliverables are never used to train general-purpose AI models and are never shared with other clients. Client production runs in enterprise-tier AI workspaces whose agreements contractually exclude customer content from model training (see Higgsfield Enterprise), and on no-training API configurations for other providers.
  • End-to-end input traceabilityImages and video are generated exclusively from rights-cleared inputs — the client's real product photography and official brand assets, ARDE-created materials, or licensed assets; never unlicensed third-party works. For every deliverable we maintain a production record documenting which inputs and prompts were used, on which model and platform, and where the human team intervened.
  • Likeness & voice consentAny use of a real person's image, likeness or voice requires prior documented consent (model release) explicitly covering AI synthesis. Consent evidence is retained and exhibitable.
  • Transparency & labelingWe disclose the use of AI in production (consistent with EU AI Act Art. 50 good practice) and identify AI-generated deliverables or elements so clients can meet platform synthetic-media labeling requirements.
  • Prohibited usesNo non-consensual deepfakes or impersonation, no deceptive or harmful misinformation, no unlawful content, no knowing infringement of third-party rights.
  • IP clarityDeliverables are assigned to the client on full payment; the jurisdiction-dependent copyright treatment of purely AI-generated elements is disclosed honestly in our Terms (§5).

(07)(Data Protection & Compliance)© 2026

Data protection & compliance

COLaw 1581/2012 EUGDPR — Art. 28 + SCCs EUAI Act transparency — Art. 50 USCCPA/CPRA service-provider terms MXLFPDPPP

In client engagements ARDE typically acts as a processor ("encargado") of any personal data contained in client materials — most commonly the images, likenesses and voices of talent, under documented consent. Our DPA covers instructions-only processing, confidentiality, security measures, sub-processor control with 30-day notice, 72-hour breach notification, audit rights, and return or deletion of data within 30 days of engagement end. We do not sell or share personal data.

ARDE maintains a documented incident-response procedure — detection, triage and severity classification, containment, eradication, recovery and post-mortem — with a commitment to notify affected clients without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirming a personal-data breach.
