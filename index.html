03/

AI governance

ARDE operates a human-directed production model: generative AI is a production tool operated under the direction, curation and final approval of human creative professionals. Our standing commitments:

  • Human review of every deliverable. No AI output reaches a client without direction, curation, editing and approval by ARDE's human team, followed by the client's own approval workflow.
  • No training with client data. Client materials, data and deliverables are never used to train general-purpose AI models and are never shared with other clients. Client production runs in enterprise-tier AI workspaces whose agreements contractually exclude customer content from model training (see Higgsfield Enterprise below), and on no-training API configurations for other providers.
  • End-to-end input traceability. Images and video are generated exclusively from rights-cleared inputs — the client's real product photography and official brand assets, ARDE-created materials, or licensed assets; never unlicensed third-party works. For every deliverable we maintain a production record documenting which inputs and prompts were used, on which model and platform, and where the human team intervened — available for the client's AI-governance records.
  • Likeness & voice consent. Any use of a real person's image, likeness or voice requires prior documented consent (model release) explicitly covering AI synthesis. Consent evidence is retained and exhibitable.
  • Transparency & labeling. We disclose the use of AI in production (consistent with EU AI Act Art. 50 good practice) and identify AI-generated deliverables or elements so clients can meet platform synthetic-media labeling requirements.
  • Prohibited uses. No non-consensual deepfakes or impersonation, no deceptive or harmful misinformation, no unlawful content, no knowing infringement of third-party rights.
  • IP clarity. Deliverables are assigned to the client on full payment; the jurisdiction-dependent copyright treatment of purely AI-generated elements is disclosed honestly in our Terms (§5).

AI generation platform — Higgsfield Enterprise

ARDE's primary generation platform for client production is Higgsfield Enterprise (Higgsfield, Inc., United States). All client work is produced exclusively within ARDE's private Enterprise workspace, governed by an enterprise agreement that supersedes Higgsfield's standard consumer terms and includes the following written commitments:

CommitmentEnterprise term
No-training guaranteeClient uploads, prompts and generated assets are never used to train Higgsfield's models or those of its model partners.
Zero rights claimedHiggsfield claims no rights over anything created on the platform; generated assets are cleared for commercial use.
Data deletionAll data deleted on request — no retention on Higgsfield's side.
Workspace securityPrivate workspace, granular access control, SSO; SOC 2-aligned controls and GDPR compliance per Higgsfield's enterprise documentation.

For verification, client legal and AI-governance teams can consult Higgsfield directly: Enterprise platform · Trust & Safety · Terms of Use · Privacy Policy. A copy of ARDE's Enterprise terms is available to client legal teams under NDA. Full provider detail in the Sub-processor & Provider List.

Data protection & compliance

CO Law 1581/2012 EU GDPR — Art. 28 + SCCs EU AI Act transparency — Art. 50 US CCPA/CPRA service-provider terms MX LFPDPPP

In client engagements ARDE typically acts as a processor ("encargado") of any personal data contained in client materials — most commonly the images, likenesses and voices of talent, under documented consent. Our DPA covers instructions-only processing, confidentiality, security measures, sub-processor control with 30-day notice, 72-hour breach notification, audit rights, and return or deletion of data within 30 days of engagement end. We do not sell or share personal data.

Incident response

ARDE maintains a documented incident-response procedure — detection, triage and severity classification, containment (including credential and token revocation), eradication, recovery and post-mortem — with a commitment to notify affected clients without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirming a personal-data breach, in line with GDPR Art. 33, Colombian SIC requirements, US state breach laws and Mexico's LFPDPPP.
