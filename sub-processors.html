Higgsfield Enterprise — platform commitments (primary generation platform)

ARDE produces client content exclusively within its Higgsfield Enterprise workspace, operated by Higgsfield, Inc. (United States; EU representative: DataRep, Rouen, France). The Enterprise tier is governed by a separate enterprise agreement whose terms supersede Higgsfield's standard consumer Terms of Use — a distinction that matters, because the standard consumer terms permit content to be used for model training, which is precisely why ARDE contracts and works at the Enterprise tier for all client production. ARDE's Enterprise plan includes the following written commitments from Higgsfield:

CommitmentWritten Enterprise term
No-training guarantee"Client uploads, prompts, and generated assets are never used to train Higgsfield or our model partners."
Zero rights claimed"Zero rights claimed over anything created on the platform." Generated assets are cleared for commercial use; ARDE's clients receive them under the IP assignment in ARDE's Terms §5.
Data deletion"All data deleted, no retention on our side" — client production data is not retained by Higgsfield after deletion.
Workspace securityPrivate enterprise workspaces with granular access control and SSO; SOC 2-aligned controls and GDPR compliance per Higgsfield's enterprise documentation.
Dedicated supportEnterprise-only support channel with direct escalation to Higgsfield's engineering team for production issues.

Consult Higgsfield directly: Enterprise platform · Trust, Safety & Platform Policies · Terms of Use · Privacy Policy. A copy of ARDE's Higgsfield Enterprise terms is available to client legal teams under NDA via contact@ardeagency.com.

Licensed software & asset libraries

Adobe Creative Cloud. ARDE's entire team operates on active commercial Adobe Creative Cloud licenses for the post-production of deliverables (editing, compositing, VFX, color, retouching and design — Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, Illustrator, among others). No deliverable is produced with unlicensed software.

Envato Elements — music and sound effects. ARDE maintains an active commercial subscription to Envato Elements (Envato Pty Ltd, Australia). Envato does not process client data — it is an inbound source of commercially licensed assets integrated into deliverables. Terms relevant to a client legal review:

AspectLicense condition
CoverageWorldwide, non-exclusive commercial license permitting use in client work (advertising video, campaigns, digital and social content).
Per-project registrationEach asset is registered/licensed for one specific end product ("single use per end product"); a new use in another project generates a new license registration. ARDE retains the license record of every delivered piece.
Client hand-overOn delivery of the end product, the client receives a limited sublicense of the asset as part of the deliverable, per Envato's terms.
Limit — broadcastThe Envato Elements music license does not cover broadcast use (TV/radio). If a deliverable is destined for broadcast, ARDE discloses this and secures a broadcast-suitable music license for that project (quoted in the applicable SOW).

Consult Envato directly: Envato Elements license terms · License FAQ.

What ARDE does not do

  • ARDE does not sell or share client personal data (as defined by the CCPA/CPRA).
  • ARDE does not use client materials or personal data to train AI models of general use, nor permit its providers to do so under the tiers it contracts for client work.
  • ARDE does not process client payment-card data; invoicing is settled by bank transfer between the parties.
  • Local production on ARDE-owned workstations (editing, compositing, color) does not leave ARDE's controlled environment.

Each sub-processor operates under contractual terms with data-protection obligations consistent with the ARDE DPA. ARDE maintains an internal vendor-assessment record and reviews providers periodically. Providers listed may vary per engagement; a client may request the exact provider set used for its projects.
